# morebuddies

A second buddy for each player in **buddy mode** (two players, each with a
computer partner: "Buddy mode" in the game options, both players have to ask
for it). `--mod morebuddies=N`, N = 1 is the original one buddy, N = 2 (the
default) gives each side a player and two buddies, six wrestlers in all.

The teams have three starting places each, so the player and two buddies fit.
The extra buddies are processes 4 (player 1's side) and 5 (player 2's), each a
random wrestler. The referee is process 6.

Tried: buddy mode with N = 2 for a short time in a screenshot: six wrestlers,
three on each side, and the game keeps running.
Unverified: a whole match; the colours of two buddies who are the same
wrestler; whether the team win check and the life bar's rewiring behave with
three on a side. The extra buddies have no getup meter, like the original
ones.
