# Cooperative ladder: two humans play the one-player championship ladder (Intercontinental: 7 matches,
# WWF: 7 matches, the last an 8-on-2) as one team against the computer's wrestlers.
#
# The game already has a cooperative mode, the royal rumble: both humans on the first team (player 2
# is put on player 1's side while royal_rumble is set), one shared life bar, one camera, and the
# computer's wrestlers one or two at a time. The mod keeps royal_rumble set during a ladder match and
# separates what is about the two humans (left as it is) from what is about the line of eight (rr_line
# in asm/COOPLADDER.ASM: the counter, the zombies, the clock, the rounds, the pin, the damage table).
# Outside a ladder match nothing changes: coop_ladder is only ever set by coop_question, and that
# needs the mod's number (coop_arg), which the machine writes only while the mod is on.
